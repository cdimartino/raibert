Privacy

Rai*bert has no accounts and sets no cookies.

Performance and play reports

The game uses Glitchless to learn how it runs and how it is played. It sends frame times, stutters, loading time and errors (including error messages and stack traces, with query strings removed from links), and what happens in the game: stages started, cleared, failed or left, what ended a life, powerups and rescues used, run results and scores, and use of the leaderboard. It also sends your difficulty, character, whether you play by keyboard or touch, your browser and its major version, operating system, whether you use a phone, a rough graphics tier, screen size and pixel ratio, the page address without its query string, and the origin of the site that linked you here.

So that returning players can be counted, the game stores a random ID in your browser and sends it with these reports. It is not tied to your name, an account or an advertising ID. Clearing this site's data removes it.

You can turn these reports off at any time: open the game menu and untick Share performance data. The game then sends nothing more to Glitchless and remembers your choice on this device. This does not delete reports already sent. Nothing is sent to Glitchless when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Bug reports

If you send a bug report (F8, or Report a bug in the menu), it contains the note you type, a picture of the game screen, your stage, recent game events and frame rates, and the device details above. The note is sent as you write it, so leave out names, email addresses and other personal details.

Site activity counts

Separately, the site counts visits, browser sessions, games started and finished, active play time and runtime errors on its own servers, with the referring site's hostname and whether you use touch or a mouse. These counts carry no visitor ID and are not sent when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. The Share performance data setting does not change them.

Leaderboard

If you choose to submit a score, your three initials, score, stage, difficulty and result are saved with a random ID for that run and shown to everyone on the leaderboard.

Kept in your browser

Your game settings and last initials, a copy of the leaderboard, a marker so a tab's visit is counted once, the Glitchless player ID and your sharing choice.
